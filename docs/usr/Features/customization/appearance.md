**Settings → UI**: theme, language and what you interact with.

## Theme

| Theme | Colors |
|-------|--------|
| **Thumbnail** (default) | Built from the cover of the song playing; light or dark follows the system. With nothing playing, album and playlist pages take their own cover's colors. |
| **System** | Follows the system's light or dark mode |
| **Wallpaper** | Follows the system's mode, with colors from your wallpaper (Android 12+) |
| **Light** / **Dark** | Always light or dark |
| **Light dynamic** / **Dark dynamic** | Always light or dark, with colors from your wallpaper |
| **Pitch black** | White text and accents on a black screen |

**Contrast**: Normal (default), Medium or High.

## Language

**Language** picks the app's language for this profile. *System* follows the device. Changing the
language in the system's app settings changes it here too.

- **Translate this language**: help finish its translation on Crowdin (hidden for System and
  English).
- **Request new language**: ask on Crowdin for a language the app doesn't have yet.

## Layout

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---------|---------|--------------|
| **Icon only** | Off | Hides button labels in menus and sheets |
| **Playlist initial** | Off | Playlists without covers show the first letter of their name instead of an icon |
| **Binary units** | On | Sizes in MiB/GiB; off for MB/GB |

## Font

| Setting | What it changes |
|---------|-----------------|
| **App font** | Every text in the app |
| **Lyrics font** | The lyrics; uses the app font until you set one |

Tap a row to pick a `.ttf` or `.otf` file. A **Font preview** opens with sample text in several
scripts (Latin with accents, CJK, right-to-left, Thai, Indic), in regular and bold. **Save** keeps
the font; going back drops it. **Reset** goes back to the default font.

Fonts are kept per profile and included in backups.

## App icon

**Launcher → App icon**: **Black** (default) or **Gradient**.
