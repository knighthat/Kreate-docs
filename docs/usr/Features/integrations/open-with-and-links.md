## Opening audio files

Choose Kreate in another app's **Open with** for an audio file (including OGG and FLAC). It plays
right away and the full player opens.

- A file that's already in your library plays as that library song.
- Any other file plays from the queue only: its tags and cover are read from the file, and it isn't
  added to your library, your history or the saved queue.

## YouTube Music links

Kreate opens `music.youtube.com` links, and YouTube Music links shared to it as text:

| Link | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| A song (`/watch`) | Plays and opens the full player |
| An album, artist or playlist | Opens its page |

Anything else shows *Couldn't open this link*.

## Sharing

**Share** in a menu shares a link:

- YouTube Music items: their `music.youtube.com` link.
- OpenSubsonic items: a share link made by your server.
- Device songs have nothing to share.
