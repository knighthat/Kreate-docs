**Details** in a song, album or artist menu looks the item up on
[MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org), the open music encyclopedia.

## Song details

Kreate finds the best matching recording by title, artist and length, and shows:

- length and first release date,
- ISRCs and genres,
- the releases the recording appears on,
- its MusicBrainz id (tap it to open musicbrainz.org).

Wrong match? Tap **Search** to open **Find the recording**, search with other words and pick the
right one.

The match is **kept** for songs in your library, so it opens straight away next time. For songs
not in your library, the page says the match isn't kept.

## Artist details

Type, area, born/formed and died/dissolved dates, aliases, genres, ISNI and links (the artist's own
site first). **Search** opens **Find the artist** to pick another match for as long as the page is
open.

## Album details

The best matching release group by title and artist.

:::note
MusicBrainz allows one request a second, so details can take a moment. Very famous songs sometimes
match a less known recording first; use Search to pick the right one.
:::
