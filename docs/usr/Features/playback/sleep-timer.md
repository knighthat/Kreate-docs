The sleep timer pauses playback after a while.

## Starting it

Tap the **sleep timer** button on the full player's Player page and choose:

- **5, 10, 15, 30, 45 minutes** or **1 hour**, or
- **End of song**: pauses when the playing song ends.

While it runs, the button shows the time left (*Pauses in 12:34*).

## Changing it

Open the menu again, or use the timer's notification:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **−** | Takes time off (Remove button step) |
| **+** | Adds time (Add button step) |
| **Pause / resume** | Holds the countdown (*On hold, 8:00 left*) |
| **Stop** | Turns the timer off |

The notification counts down silently. Swiping it away doesn't stop the timer.

## When it runs out

Playback pauses and a notification says so. Its **Restart** button starts the same timer again and
resumes playing.

The timer is dropped when the queue is cleared.

## Settings

**Settings → Player → Sleep timer**:

| Setting | Default | Choices |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Remove button** | 5 minutes | 1, 2, 5, 10 minutes, or until the song ends |
| **Add button** | 5 minutes | 1, 2, 5, 10 minutes, or until the song ends |

*Until the song ends* turns the timer into **End of song** either way.
