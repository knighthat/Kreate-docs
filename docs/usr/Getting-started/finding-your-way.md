## The main screen

The bottom bar has the four library tabs: **Songs**, **Albums**, **Artists** and **Playlists**.
See [Home tabs](../Features/library/home.md).

The top bar has three buttons:

| Button | Opens |
|--------|-------|
| **Search** | [Search](../Features/library/search.md) |
| **Profile** | Your Account page: History, Statistics, Liked, Disliked, Downloaded and Switch profile |
| **Settings** | Every setting, see the [settings map](../Features/reference/settings.md) |

Each tab's toolbar also has a button that opens a sheet with that tab's actions (play all, shuffle,
open a random item…) and filters.

## The mini player

While something is queued, the mini player sits above the bottom bar.

- **Tap** or **drag it up** to open the [full player](../Features/playback/player.md).
- **Drag it down** past half its height, or fling it down, to stop playback and clear the queue.

## Long press

Long press any song, album, artist or playlist to open its menu. The item lifts out of the list
and the menu slides up under it. See [Song menu](../Features/library/song-menu.md).

## Badges

Small badges on covers tell you about an item at a glance:

- **Source**: where the item comes from (device, YouTube Music, OpenSubsonic).
- **Explicit**: the song is marked explicit.
- **Downloaded** or **Cached**: the song is kept on the device
  (see [Downloads](../Features/offline/downloads.md)).
- **Bookmarked / followed** albums and artists.

## Going back

By default, going back from the first screen closes the app. Under
**Settings → General → Navigation** you can keep it open instead, or ask for a confirmation
(press back twice within a few seconds).

## Messages

Short messages appear at the bottom of the screen, for example after removing a song from a
playlist. Many have an **Undo** button.
